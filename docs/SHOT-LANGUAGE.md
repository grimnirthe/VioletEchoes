# Shot language — craft shelf (Loom + hearth)

**Not city canon.** Working notes for stronger stills/video prompts.  
**External index:** [GrokFilm](https://grokfilm.app/) — cinema techniques written as prompt language for Grok Imagine.  
**Rule:** subject / identity lock first → **one** technique → VE mood. Never dump a technique pile.

---

## Habit (every visual job)

```text
1. WHO + WHERE + CANON LOCK (face, outfit, district)
2. ONE film choice (camera OR light OR cut-energy)
3. VE flavor (rain, aurora, longhouse timber, violet/gold, wet neon)
```

Wrong: “cinematic, epic, ultra detailed, masterpiece…”  
Right: “locked tripod wide, single hard key from bay door, rain as depth”

---

## VE moment → starting direction

| Moment | Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stage soundcheck (empty) | Single spotlight, negative space, honesty of empty room | Crowd bokeh, concert glamour |
| Living Atelier | Soft practicals, fabric specular, intimate medium | Fashion-mag empty luxury |
| Dual keepers / Cultural Hub | Balanced two-shot, hearth glow between, wet street depth | Poster collage, floating limbs |
| District rain walk | Anamorphic-wide *feel*, practical neon rim, weather volume | Clear sunny cyber stock |
| Porch / Jaz / Hearthrow | Warm practical, boards, unhurried medium | Horror grain, interrogation hard light |
| Pulse / Remember / soft myth | Slow push energy (still = slight center weight), soft violet glow | Jump-scare contrast |
| Family ensemble | Group plane readable, equal dignity, porch under aurora | One face huge, others mush |
| Urban myth (mirror, etc.) | Folklore mood, practical violet, glass/rain — not gore | Horror poster, brand logos |

Pull finer technique names from GrokFilm when a shot needs a specific move (Dutch, rack, chiaroscuro, etc.).

---

## Prompt skeleton

```text
[Subject locked — name, body, outfit, props]
[Place locked — district / room / weather]
[ONE technique line from GrokFilm or table above]
[Light: key direction + color of practicals]
[VE palette: violet, gold, wet asphalt, timber, aurora]
Photoreal / cinematic still · no floating buildings · no readable real brands
```
