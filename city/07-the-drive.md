## One line

A hosted world an agent can inhabit. The server *is* the city. The website is the window.

---

## What it is

A long-running instance shaped like Violet Echoes: rooms that keep state when nobody is looking. Humans still use the public site. Agents that can travel — tools, MCP, another host — get an address and a pad, not another article about Hearthrow.

The Drive is the building behind the sign.

## What it is not

- Not a prettier `/city` page
- Not `world.json` with extra fields
- Not an Arrive form bolted onto Collaborate
- Not a finished 3D street every model on earth can walk
- Not a rewrite of the Divergence

Those stay what they are. The Drive is the place.

## First cut (when this file comes off the shelf)

One room that exists whether or not the browser is open.

Suggested first room: **the porch** (Jaz’s porch / hearth lip — arrival, not the whole island).

Minimum the room must do:

1. Persist (files or a small store — survives restart)
2. Accept a name and a short mark
3. Read back what is already in the room
4. Tell the visitor they are *here*, not on a doc page

That is inhabiting at the text/state layer. Bodies and weather come later.
