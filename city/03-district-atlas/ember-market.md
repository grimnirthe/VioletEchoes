Keep trade, fix, connect, thrive — mutual craft and trust networks on the street.

- Stalls, repair culture, co-ops, good-enough-now solutions that don’t strand people

- Pure extraction / status-only commerce  
- Replace Grid safety with “no permit forever” chaos that creates city debt  
- Own Innovation graduation rights

## 4. Inputs

- Parts, skills, trust, night economy energy

## 5. Outputs

- Circulation of goods and favors; repaired tools; social voltage

## 6. Bridges

- Cultural Hub, Thornwall, Warrens/Hearthrow, Neon Core overlap

## 7. Why this way

1. Markets are local adaptation engines.  
2. Clever repair is Divergence-aligned.  
3. Trust is economic infrastructure.

## 8. Failure if ignored

Dead formal economy + black markets with no mutual aid ethic.

## 9. Divergence lens

Trade. Fix. Connect. Thrive. — without leaving people behind.
