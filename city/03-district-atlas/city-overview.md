# Dual-Layer City — Overview

**Edge Node:** n/a (whole-island frame)  
**Layer:** Both  
**Related:** city-overview, districts  

## 1. Mandate

Hold two truths on one island: **capability** (systems) and **soul** (lived character) in continuous dialogue.

## 2. Builds

- Clear dual-layer maps for writers, builders, and researchers  
- Scenes where both layers are visible in the same frame  
- Decision checks: “Does this strengthen capability, soul, or the seam between them?”

## 3. Does not

- Split into two disconnected cities  
- Let systems erase human mess  
- Let nostalgia erase maintenance and power

## 4. Inputs

- District specs, Edge Node styles, longhouse patterns, Divergence constraints

## 5. Outputs

- Coherent geography; story tension between Functional and Lived nodes; site `/city` narrative

## 6. Bridges

- All districts; Eimyrja; Bifrost bridges; family seats at intersections

## 7. Why this way

1. Pure machine cities feel empty; pure village fantasy cannot host neuromorphic infrastructure.  
2. Divergence requires local life *and* energy-honest systems.  
3. Family longhouses make sense only if they sit on a real city, not a void backdrop.

## 8. Failure if ignored

World becomes either tech brochure or character sheet with no ground. Researchers cannot answer “where does X live and why?”

## 9. Divergence lens

Not two cities — two layers, one echo. Capability without soul is empty; soul without capability cannot endure.
