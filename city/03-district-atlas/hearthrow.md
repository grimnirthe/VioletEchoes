# Hearthrow

**Edge Node:** Warmth  
**Layer:** Lived  
**Related:** hearthrow, warrens  

## 1. Mandate

Keep life going — together: porches, tea, stories, soft social fabric of ordinary days.

## 2. Builds

- Multi-generational presence; check-ins; shared meals; memory warmed in conversation  
- Resistance to loneliness-as-policy

## 3. Does not

- Optimize households into silence  
- Perform busyness as virtue  
- Replace Warmth with pure surveillance “care”

## 4. Inputs

- People showing up; food; time; neighborhood attention

## 5. Outputs

- Belonging; reduced isolation; practiced continuity

## 6. Bridges

- Warrens Living Grid, Cultural Hub, Longhouses, Green Spaces

## 7. Why this way

1. Soft social infrastructure is how longhouses scale beyond one family.  
2. Stories are memory maintenance.  
3. The city must have a place that is allowed to be ordinary.

## 8. Failure if ignored

High-tech loneliness with perfect utilities.

## 9. Divergence lens

We keep life going — together. Tea and stories always welcome.
