# Eimyrja & Data Spines

**Systems:** Eimyrja (heartwood), Data Spines (coherence arteries)  
**Layer:** Functional (felt in lived layer as hum, fountain hue, pulse)  
**Related:** eimyrja, data-spines, edge-nodes  

## 1. Mandate

Keep multi-generational **coherence** without becoming an overlord; carry **lossy, energy-visible** signals rather than total surveillance pipes.

## 2. Builds

- Recommendations and soft constraints from abstracted Edge summaries  
- Visible spine cost of high-fidelity demand  
- Circadian / maintenance culture (Deep Sleep, regeneration cycles)  
- Runic flow mappings (Ansuz, Uruz, Laguz, Thurisaz, Eihwaz, Sowilo) as functional aesthetics

## 3. Does not

- Issue continuous hard command over routine life  
- Keep a perfect real-time twin of everything  
- Treat unlimited bandwidth as free  
- Replace human reservation on foundational choices

## 4. Inputs

- Lossy Edge Node tokens; energy budgets; crisis escalations; cultural warming signals

## 5. Outputs

- Pattern alerts; soft guidance; institutional memory at city scale; the felt “city breathing”

## 6. Bridges

- Every Edge Node; Public Services; Innovation (firewalled); External Relations / Veilreach

## 7. Why this way

1. Central command recreates the scaling-and-oversight trap.  
2. Lossiness protects energy and prevents brittle total coupling.  
3. Citizens should feel coordination without feeling micromanaged.

## 8. Failure if ignored

Either god-AI fanfic (control theater) or dead infrastructure (no heartbeat). Trust collapses either way.

## 9. Divergence lens

Recommend, don’t dominate. Escalation is expensive. Heartwood, not throne.
