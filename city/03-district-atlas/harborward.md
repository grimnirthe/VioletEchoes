# Harborward

**Edge Node:** Old Tide  
**Layer:** Lived  
**Related:** harborward  

## 1. Mandate

Keep the working harbor honest: wet logistics, weather, living ships, and crews who come home late.

## 2. Builds

- Berths, breakwater practice, dockside longhouses, storm continuity  
- Respect for living vessels and old hulls sharing water

## 3. Does not

- Sand grit into postcard port  
- Fair-weather-only systems  
- Treat crews as expendable throughput

## 4. Inputs

- Weather, cargo, ship bonds, crew needs

## 5. Outputs

- Steady arrivals; harbor memory; safe berths

## 6. Bridges

- Living ships, Public Services, Veilreach (edge), Thornwall (repair)

## 7. Why this way

1. Ports teach continuity under real stress.  
2. Living ships need wet truth, not pure sim.  
3. “Steady when late” is moral infrastructure.

## 8. Failure if ignored

Show harbor with no crews — lore without logistics.

## 9. Divergence lens

Technology rides grit; it does not sand it away.
