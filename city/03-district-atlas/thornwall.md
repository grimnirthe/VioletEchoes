# Thornwall — Old Iron

**Edge Node:** Old Iron  
**Layer:** Lived (industrial / maintenance)  
**Related:** thornwall  

## 1. Mandate

Keep hard industry and maintenance culture: **built hard, kept hard**, respect earned in the repair.

## 2. Builds

- Repairable systems; spare knowledge; crew loyalty; work-frames  
- Graduation landing zone for things that must survive real wear

## 3. Does not

- Disposable infrastructure fashion  
- Status without service  
- Mock slow maintenance as “not innovative”

## 4. Inputs

- Broken things; parts; skilled labor; honest failure reports

## 5. Outputs

- Running machines; intergenerational craft; fewer future debts

## 6. Bridges

- Public Services, Innovation (graduation), Ember Market, Harborward

## 7. Why this way

1. Maintenance is defense.  
2. Easy-to-fix beats hard-to-admit-broke.  
3. City romance dies if nobody turns the wrench.

## 8. Failure if ignored

Pretty systems that cannot be repaired — collapse with style.

## 9. Divergence lens

Hard to break. Easy to fix. Respect is earned in the repair.
