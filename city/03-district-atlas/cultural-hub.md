# Cultural Hub — Heart-Public / Memory Commons / Stage District

**Edge Node personality:** Expressive, relational, hates hollow spectacle  
**Layer:** Functional + public soul  
**Related:** cultural-hub, memory  

## 1. Mandate

Host shared culture, living memory, education, and ritual in public — **resonance over empty performance**.

## 2. Builds

- Stages and commons people return to  
- Memory-warming practices (story, re-enactment, song, drill)  
- Clear retirement of empty stages

## 3. Does not

- Force private longhouse intimacy into public product  
- Preserve hollow tourism as “culture”  
- Own Innovation kill-switches or Grid ops  
- Silence Velora/Star work seats by absorbing them without bridge

## 4. Inputs

- Practiced arts; family-safe public works; education; ritual calendars

## 5. Outputs

- Warmed memory; shared identity; attenuated empty spectacle

## 6. Bridges

- Ember Market, Spire, family seats, Memory practices, music yard (ops)

## 7. Why this way

1. Divergence memory is use-based; culture is maintenance.  
2. Public soul must not become surveillance of private hearths.  
3. Resonance scales belonging without central creed.

## 8. Failure if ignored

Museum freeze or content farm — neither warms what keeps the city itself.

## 9. Divergence lens

Not spectacle. Resonance. We are the resonance we choose to carry.
