# Green Spaces — The Lungs / Heatsink / Soft Grid

**Edge Node personality:** Patient, long-horizon, protective of slow processes  
**Layer:** Functional (felt as lived restoration)  
**Related:** green-spaces  

## 1. Mandate

Hold ecological regulation, cooling, air/water quality, and psychological restoration as **load-bearing infrastructure**, not decoration.

## 2. Builds

- Living cooling loops; restorative parks; long-horizon health metrics  
- Seasonal / cyclic planning  
- Resistance to extractive “pretty for the brochure” builds

## 3. Does not

- Accept pure cosmetic greenery that fails as infrastructure  
- Run on quarterly dashboards alone  
- Yield lungs to unlimited densification without compensation

## 4. Inputs

- Heat load; water; biodiversity signals; human restoration need

## 5. Outputs

- Cooler districts; cleaner cycles; places that return people to themselves

## 6. Bridges

- Hearthrow, Harborward, Cultural Hub, Public Services, Residential fabric

## 7. Why this way

1. Under Divergence, ecology is not amenity — it is system.  
2. Slow processes outlast clever spikes.  
3. Psych restoration is part of city coherence.

## 8. Failure if ignored

Heat death of comfort, decorative dead parks, or burnout with no place to breathe.

## 9. Divergence lens

We do not decorate the world. We carry its breath.
