# World Bible — Short Spine

This is the **index card**, not the whole shelf.

## One paragraph

Violet Echoes is a dual-layer neuromorphic city on a digital island, built on **The Development Divergence**: coherence over peak spikes, energy honesty, local Edge intelligence, memory through use, cultivation over pure control. **Eimyrja** recommends; **Edge Nodes** live in districts; **Longhouses** hold human-scale belonging; the **Violet Longhouse Nexus** is roots, not a throne.
