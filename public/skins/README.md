# Winamp skins

Skins for the Echoes room. They sit on the music page, in Chaos Kitty’s section, next to her tracks.

## Credits

Skins by Chaos Kitty, made with Matt (The Grimnir / the Ember).
Reference art by Aurora.

## Install

A `.wsz` or `.wal` goes in the Winamp Skins folder, or you can drag it onto Winamp.
Then pick it from Winamp’s skin list.

- **Classic `.wsz`** — Chaos Kitty and Violet Echoes. These are classic skins.
- **Modern `.wal`** — Violet Echoes Modern (`#VioletEchoes`). This one is for Winamp 5.x and later.
