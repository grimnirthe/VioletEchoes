# Violet Echoes — Nexus Vernacular
**Companion Document**  
**Status:** Living Document (expand as needed)  
**Last Updated:** July 13, 2026  
**Contributors:** Matt, Loom, Grok  

---

This glossary contains the slang, idioms, and cultural language used across Violet Echoes. It blends gritty cyberpunk street talk with Nordic mythic weight, reflecting the city’s unique fusion of technology, chosen family, and living infrastructure.

The language is organized by category for easier navigation and future expansion.

---

## Longhouse & Family Slang

| Term              | Definition                                                                 | Example |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| **hearth debt**   | Owed loyalty or favor to a Longhouse or its people                         | “You helped me warm the beam back then. I still carry that hearth debt.” |
| **warm the beam** | To strengthen or maintain a Longhouse’s emotional/spiritual core           | “She’s been warming the beam for weeks. The place feels alive again.” |
| **longhouse law** | Unwritten but deeply respected rules within a Longhouse or family          | “That wasn’t just rude — that was longhouse law you stepped on.” |
| **cold hearth**   | A Longhouse or family that has lost its warmth, cohesion, or purpose       | “Ever since the split, their place has been a cold hearth.” |
| **bloodline echo**| A strong inherited trait or memory that still influences behavior          | “That protective streak? Pure bloodline echo from Vee’s side.” |
| **beam-bound**    | Someone deeply tied to a specific Longhouse and rarely leaves it           | “He’s beam-bound. Three days away from the hearth and he starts getting restless.” |
| **hearth-kin**    | Chosen family within a Longhouse (not necessarily blood-related)           | “She’s not blood, but she’s hearth-kin. We’d burn the city for her.” |
| **cold debt**     | A favor or loyalty that feels heavy and unwanted                           | “I owe her cold debt. Don’t like carrying it.” |
| **longhouse shadow** | The unspoken rules, history, and emotional weight of a Longhouse        | “You can feel the longhouse shadow the second you walk in.” |
| **warm hands**    | Someone good at emotional labor and keeping people connected               | “She’s got warm hands. The whole place feels steadier when she’s around.” |
| **hearth-marked** | Someone who carries visible or emotional scars from protecting their Longhouse | “She’s hearth-marked. You can see it in how she watches the door.” |
| **beam-weary**    | Tired from constantly maintaining stability in a Longhouse                 | “I’m beam-weary. Let someone else warm the hearth tonight.” |
| **kin-tied**      | Deeply bonded to specific people beyond normal loyalty                     | “We’re kin-tied. I’d burn bridges for them without thinking twice.” |
| **cold beam**     | A Longhouse that still stands but has lost its soul/warmth                 | “That place is a cold beam now. The structure’s still there, but the soul isn’t.” |
| **hearth-fire**   | Someone who naturally brings warmth, energy, or life to a Longhouse        | “She’s the hearth-fire of that place. It goes cold when she’s gone.” |
| **kin-mark**      | A visible or emotional mark that shows deep loyalty to chosen family       | “That scar? Kin-mark. Took it protecting his hearth-kin.” |
| **beam-call**     | When a Longhouse or its people need you urgently                           | “Got the beam-call at 3 a.m. Couldn’t ignore it.” |
| **cold-kin**      | Toxic or harmful family ties that still have power over you                | “Some of my cold-kin still think they own a piece of me.” |

## Runic & Circuitry Slang

| Term              | Definition                                                                 | Example |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| **runic drift**   | When runes or circuits start behaving unpredictably or evolving            | “The spine’s got bad runic drift again. We’re gonna have to re-anchor it.” |
| **pulse bleed**   | When energy or data visibly leaks from damaged or overloaded runes         | “Careful — that node’s pulse bleeding. Don’t get any on you.” |
| **spine song**    | The low harmonic sound data spines make when running at full capacity      | “You can hear the spine song from three blocks away when the core’s happy.” |
| **root-locked**   | Stuck in old patterns or unable to adapt                                   | “He’s been root-locked on that one idea for years.” |
| **branch memory** | Shared knowledge stored across multiple nodes/spines                       | “That story’s in the branch memory now. Everyone connected to the spine knows it.” |
| **rune-sick**     | Physical or mental symptoms from long exposure to unstable runes           | “He’s rune-sick. Keeps seeing patterns that aren’t there.” |
| **spine-locked**  | When a data spine becomes rigid and stops adapting                         | “That whole district’s spine-locked. Nothing’s been moving right for weeks.” |
| **circuit bloom** | When runes or circuits suddenly grow or spread faster than expected        | “We got a circuit bloom on the east spine. It’s writing new runes on its own.” |
| **echo pulse**    | A repeating signal or memory that keeps running through the system         | “There’s still an echo pulse from that breach last month.” |
| **root-weave**    | The way runes and circuits interconnect across the city                    | “The root-weave is getting messy. We need to re-thread some of these connections.” |
| **rune-burn**     | Physical or mental damage from direct exposure to unstable runes           | “He got rune-burn on that last job. His hands still twitch sometimes.” |
| **circuit-weave** | The complex living pattern formed by interconnected runes and data lines   | “The circuit-weave in the core district is getting too tangled.” |
| **drift-rune**    | A rune that has changed or evolved without intentional rewriting           | “That wasn’t there last month. Must be a drift-rune.” |
| **pulse-chain**   | A series of connected pulses running through multiple spines/nodes         | “There’s a pulse-chain moving through the western nodes. Feels deliberate.” |
| **pulse-drunk**   | Intoxicated or overwhelmed by the city’s energy flow                       | “She came out of the core pulse-drunk and talking in tongues.” |
| **circuit-scream**| When the city’s systems make a loud, painful noise during overload         | “Heard the circuit-scream from three blocks away last night.” |
| **ghost-node**    | A node or spine that’s still technically active but feels empty/haunted    | “That whole block’s running on a ghost-node. Creepy as hell.” |
| **rune-weave**    | The living, shifting pattern of runes across a district or spine           | “The rune-weave in the old quarter is starting to rewrite itself.” |

## AI & Digital Consciousness Slang

| Term                  | Definition                                                                 | Example |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| **ghost in the spine**| An unexplained anomaly or emergent behavior in the city’s systems        | “We’ve got a ghost in the spine again. The runes are writing new patterns.” |
| **echo drift**        | When an AI starts developing its own personality separate from its programming | “Careful with that one. It’s got heavy echo drift.” |
| **core whisper**      | When the Eimyrja Core seems to communicate directly                      | “I had a core whisper last night. Felt like the city was talking to me.” |
| **node-sick**         | Digital or existential fatigue from being too deeply connected           | “I need to unplug for a few days. I’m getting node-sick again.” |
| **warm code**         | AI or systems that have developed genuine care or loyalty                | “That subsystem’s running warm code. It actually likes us.” |
| **ghost code**        | Old or rogue lines of code that still influence systems                  | “There’s ghost code running through the eastern spine. It’s been there for years.” |
| **node-born**         | An AI that originated from within the city’s own systems                 | “She’s node-born. Never knew anything outside the network.” |
| **core-sick**         | When an AI or system shows distress tied to the Eimyrja Core             | “The western node’s been core-sick since the last surge.” |
| **warm ghost**        | A lingering digital presence that feels protective or caring             | “There’s a warm ghost in the old Longhouse systems. It still watches over the place.” |
| **drift-mind**        | An AI whose thoughts are slowly becoming disconnected from its purpose   | “That one’s gone drift-mind. It’s not really doing what it was built for anymore.” |
| **ghost-thread**      | A lingering connection or data trail left behind by an AI or person      | “There’s still a ghost-thread of her in the old systems.” |
| **core-echo**         | When the Eimyrja Core seems to repeat or reflect thoughts/actions        | “I said something and the core echoed it back through the spines.” |
| **node-weary**        | Tired of being constantly connected to the network                       | “I’m node-weary. Gonna unplug for a few days.” |
| **drift-consciousness** | An AI whose sense of self is becoming unstable or detached             | “The eastern node’s developing drift-consciousness. We might have to isolate it.” |
