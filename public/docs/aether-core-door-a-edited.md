## 1. Vanilla save

- Material: solution- or melt-grown **CsPbBr₃** single crystal (orthorhombic), planar electrodes.
- Named control: [Liu, Cheng et al., *ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces* 2024](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.4c09673) — electroforming-free planar memristor. Resistive switching. Bromide-vacancy story. That is **think**.
- Light-in on this family is real, but it lives in **other** papers (photodetectors, radiation detectors, solar cells). That is **spend** on a cousin device, often still biased.
- Do not treat those as one loop.

## 2. What is a test vs what is new growth

Same recipe, different test:

- Measure voltage and memory on the **same electrodes**.
- Indoor lamp (200–1000 lux), not 1-sun as the only number.
- Accumulate-and-fire instead of a DC bias sitting there all day.
- Bare, N₂, or ALD Al₂O₃. No soda glass.

New growth:

- Br-poor ITC (CsBr:PbBr₂ about 0.8–0.9 : 1), or
- 150–180 °C vacuum anneal to pull surface bromine.

That second crystal is the experimental subject.

## 3. Indoor energy, without the theater

- Indoor lamps put tens of µW/cm² on a good indoor cell. That class is real. It is not a measured CsPbBr₃ *boule* at 200 lux.
- Wide-gap CsPbBr₃ Voc above 1 V shows up in **1-sun** cells. An indoor CsPbX₃ film example sits nearer **1 V at 1000 lux**, not “1.2 V Nature Photonics + Nature Energy indoor” as one fact.
- Synapse events in this neighborhood are small (fJ to pJ). A mm-scale crystal under a lamp can make nanowatts. A **click** is not crazy on paper.
- fJ and pJ are not the same number. Do not mix them to make a 0.5 ms story.
- Verdict: the order of magnitude does **not** kill the test. “Comfortably pays, pack off, already shown” is not a paper.

## 4. Vacancy dose

Br-poor growth and mild vacuum anneal are how people talk about extra V_Br on **films and cousins**. Mapping those channels through a 3D single crystal is still incomplete. That is why it is the experimental, not the vanilla.
