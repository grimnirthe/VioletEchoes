## What we are asking

We named a **target node**: one piece of matter that does three jobs in the same volume.

1. **Think** — ionic / vacancy memory (resistive switching, synapse-like state).
2. **Spend** — incoming light is the current. No wall pack required to switch when lit.
3. **Beat** — a rhythm from strain / polarity (piezo or ferro), not a glued quartz watch.

Old machines split those into CPU, PSU, and clock. We are asking whether a real lattice (or a tightly coupled two-family node) can close that loop **in theory**, and what a lab would actually measure.

This is a hunt, not a part number.

## What we already believe (correct us)

- Architecture is named. Single lattice that does all three at room light has **not** shipped.
- Halide perovskite (ABX₃) is the right neighborhood for think + spend. Oxide perovskite (BaTiO₃ family) is the right neighborhood for beat.
- Coupling a solar film to a memristor to a piezo disc is a sandwich. That is the old split with extra glue.
- Violet is bandgap, not paint. Defer color. Do not optimize for purple first.
- Support chemistry **Ca · Si · O · Al · Na** (quartz / zeolite ancestry) is a **shell / binder / scaffold**, not the brain. Working tag: `VE CaSiOAlNa`.
- PCM / GST chips are a different street (heat-written, powered). Do not glue them onto this.

Starter halide space we already wrote (not a SKU):

**CsₓFA₁₋ₓPb(BrᵧI₁₋ᵧ)₃** — Cs-rich enough to hold phase, Br high enough to leave the black solar strip, grown slightly iodine-poor so vacancies exist on purpose. Gap window we aimed at: **~2.3–2.8 eV**.

If Pb is barred, the fallback is a halide double (Cs₂AgBiBr₆ family) — more stable, weaker synapse. Record the loss.

## Questions. Answer in this order.

**1. Energy loop**  
Under indoor / window light (no UV gun as the only fuel), can light-in plus strain-in pay for ion motion and a measurable rhythm, or does this class of stack still need a bias? Order-of-magnitude is fine. Say if you cannot estimate.

**2. Same volume vs two families**  
Is “one boule, three jobs” physically plausible in one halide, or is dual-family (oxide ring + halide core) the only honest path? Do not split the difference to be nice.

**3. What a calculation would actually compute**  
List the DFT / continuum runs a real group would do: bandgap vs Br fraction, vacancy migration barrier, piezoelectric / ferroelectric coefficients, photovoltage vs gap, energy in vs energy out. Do not fake the outputs.

**4. Support shell**  
Does wrapping or scaffolding with Ca-Si-O-Al-Na (zeolite-like or silica-like) help stability / ion paths, or does it just bury the crystal? Point at perovskite–zeolite papers if you know them. No mythology.

**5. Pass / fail against our card**  
Our card, on **one** sample, air, window-light:

- open-circuit voltage appears (spend)
- resistance state changes with history and still reads later (think)
- a strain or photo-strain rhythm couples to one of those (beat)
- pull the external supply; it does not go mute in ordinary indoor blue
- a week later it still does those three

Which of those does the literature already touch? Which is the wall?

**6. Who would test it**  
Name the *kind* of lab (halide single-crystal growth, piezo-phototronics, optoelectronic memristors). Not a company pitch. Not “email me.”
