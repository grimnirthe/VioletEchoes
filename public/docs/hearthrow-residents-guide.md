## 1. The Soul of the District: Nordic Longhouse Ethics

Welcome home. Your journey in Hearthrow begins at the intersection of the glowing digital sea and the grounding warmth of old wood. As a resident, you are no longer a mere data point in the Violet Echoes metropolis; you are part of a *living nexus*. Here, the high-tech grit of the city meets the **Nordic Longhouse soul** — a communal shield designed to protect our collective humanity from the friction of raw scaling.

In Hearthrow, we live by the **Development Divergence**. While the rest of the world chases peak performance, we prioritize coherence. You are here to embody three primary values that serve as our district’s counter-signal to the digital storm:

- **Family:** This is your primary anchor. In our longhouse ethics, family is the shared roof that ensures no one faces the digital rain alone. It is the foundational unit of our nexus.
- **Freedom:** The city provides the architecture, but you provide the movement. You have the agency to explore and evolve, provided your path respects the shared resonance of the district.
- **Love:** This is the binding force of the Eimyrja system. It is the commitment that every resident is seen, heard, valued, and loved by both their neighbors and the systems that sustain them.

Hearthrow’s Edge Node callsign remains **Warmth**. Tea and stories always welcome. The values above sit under that roof — they do not replace it.

## 2. The Porch Light Protocol: Connection Over Control

As you walk the rain-lit streets of our metropolis, look to the doorsteps. In Hearthrow, we favor **Connection over control**. You will notice the porch lights — warm amber glows that cut through the violet neon. These are not merely for illumination; they are active signals. A lit porch is a declaration of availability, a silent invitation to a neighbor that the hearth is open and the bond is ready to be strengthened.

### The Recommendation Standard

We reject the *command culture* of rigid urban grids. In Hearthrow, we operate on a **recommendation culture**. Whether it is a neighbor suggesting a repair or the Eimyrja system suggesting a power shift, we prioritize belonging and relation before command. We do not tell you what to do; we show you what the community needs to remain coherent.
